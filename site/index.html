Overview

Built for producers who want control fast.

Load a drum sample, shape how it reacts, clean or rebuild parts of the source, then push the result into something more playable. YOURDRUM™ is small on purpose: fast decisions, obvious controls, low price.

Main editor

Shape the sample before it hits the mix.

The sample view is the center of YOURDRUM™. Load a one-shot, shape the transient, and let velocity control how aggressive the hit feels.

  • Designed for quick drum shaping without building a complicated rack.
  • Lower velocity can shorten the transient, so softer hits feel tighter instead of just quieter.
  • Useful for hats, snares, percussion, shakers and one-shot drum layers.
Cleanup

Spectral Gate

Threshold, amount and FFT size cut rough frequency junk before the hit gets pushed harder.

Motion

Phase Distortion

Amount, bias and mix bend the phase feel of the drum, giving the transient movement without plain clipping.

Character

Sploinger

Freq and amount add an elastic snap to the hit, so the one-shot feels shaped instead of simply played back.

Rebuild

Resynthesis

Rebuilds the source into a cleaner or more synthetic version while keeping the original drum identity.

Niche engine

A drum sampler with a shaper people actually need.

Built around a focused one-shot shaper, YOURDRUM™ does not just trigger the sample - it changes how the hit behaves, including transient length, body, tone and movement.

01 / One-shot first

No generic sampler behavior.

YOURDRUM™ is made for drum one-shots, not full instruments. Drop in a kick, snare, hat or shaker and shape the hit directly.

02 / Velocity shaper

Softer hits can get shorter.

When velocity goes down, the transient can be shortened, so ghost notes and softer hits feel controlled instead of weak.

03 / Buy reason

A focused shaping workflow.

The idea is simple: one-shot shaping that would normally take a sampler, envelope work, transient tools and extra FX - here it is built into one focused drum plugin.
